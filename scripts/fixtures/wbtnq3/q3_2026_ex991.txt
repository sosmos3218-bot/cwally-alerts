WEBTOON Entertainment Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
today announced results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter Revenue Decline of 4.0% ; Revenue Growth on a Constant Currency Basis of 0.2%
Total revenue of $362.8 million decreased 4.0%, driven by declines in Paid Content and IP Adaptations, partially offset by growth in Advertising.
Revenue on a constant currency basis was $378.6 million, increasing 0.2%, driven by growth in Paid Content and Advertising, partially offset by a decline in IP Adaptations.
For the fourth quarter 2026, the Company expects: Revenue growth on a constant currency basis in the range of 9.0%-12.0%. This represents revenue in the range of $371-$381 million, based on current FX rates.
Revenue Growth on a Constant Currency Basis: We define revenue growth on a constant currency basis as period-over-period growth rates of revenue, adjusted to remove the impact of foreign currency rate fluctuations and the impact of deconsolidated and transferred operations.
